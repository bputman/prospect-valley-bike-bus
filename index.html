Every school morning

The bike bus is rolling

A group of neighborhood kids and grown-ups riding bikes to school together, on a set route, at a set time. Safer than riding alone, way more fun than the car line.

Join the ride See the route

What it is

One route, one time, a whole crowd of bikes

Think of it like a school bus — except everyone's pedaling. Families join in along a fixed route at set stops, and we all ride in together as a group, led by volunteer adults.

01

Safety in numbers

Riding as a visible group is safer than riding solo — drivers see us coming, and adult volunteers ride at the front and back.

02

Skip the car line

No idling, no parking scramble. Kids roll straight up to the bike rack, wide awake and ready for the day.

03

Genuinely fun

Music, bubbles, decorated bikes, and friends the whole way. Most kids ask to ride again the next day.
